## 👋 About Me

Hi! I'm Wenxi Chen (陈文熙), a first-year Ph.D. student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Shanghai Innovation Institute. I am a member of the [X-Lance Lab](https://x-lance.sjtu.edu.cn), advised by Prof. [Xie Chen](https://chenxie95.github.io/). I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the IEEE Pilot Class at SJTU in 2025.

My research focuses on understanding, generation, and interaction across general audio (including speech, audio, and music), as well as multimodal large language models. My recent work spans speech representation modeling (SSL and codecs), audio understanding, zero-shot TTS, end-to-end spoken dialogue systems, and general music editing.
