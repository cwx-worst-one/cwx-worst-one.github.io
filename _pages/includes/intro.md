## 👋 About Me

Hi! I'm Wenxi Chen (陈文熙), a first-year Ph.D. student at the [X-Lance Lab](https://x-lance.sjtu.edu.cn), Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), under the supervision of Prof. [Xie Chen](https://chenxie95.github.io/). I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science (IEEE Pilot Class) from SJTU in 2025.

My research focuses on understanding, generation, and interaction across general audio (including speech, audio, and music), as well as multimodal large language models. My recent work spans speech representation modeling (SSL and codecs), audio understanding (acoustic scene classification and captioning), zero-shot TTS, end-to-end spoken dialogue systems, and general music editing.
